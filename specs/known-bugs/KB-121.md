## KB-121 — Regenerating one episode's outline ignores its neighbouring episodes

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #405.** The `season-outline` job carries
> `surroundingEpisodes` and `additionalContext` (the writer's note, which was
> dropped the same way). The handler formats them
> (`formatNeighbouringEpisodes`, sanitised, in episode order) and the Season
> Outliner passes them to `season-outline` (v3, new `surrounding_episodes`
> section) from the job, not from the LLM's tool arguments. Red on the base
> branch: `episodes/__tests__/neighbouring-episodes.test.ts` 2 of 4 (the
> other two cover the new formatter), plus the producer and handler cases
> (guards NE1–NE4).
>
> **Reach:** `regenerateEpisodeOutlineAction` has no caller in the app today
> (only tests), so no user sees this until a control calls it. Found while
> here: the outliner sends characters, locations and recurring elements under
> names the prompt does not read, so every outline goes out with literal
> `{{characters}}` in it — filed as KB-126.

**Severity:** Low — a regenerated outline can contradict the episodes around
it; nothing is lost. **Found:** KB-33's producer audit (worker-authz, #384).

`regenerateEpisodeOutline`
(`packages/features/episodes/src/server/batch-episode-actions.ts`) sent
`surroundingEpisodes` in a `season-outline` job so that a single episode is
written to fit between its neighbours. The handler
(`apps/web/lambda/llm-worker/handlers/season-outline.ts`) has no such field
and never passes them to the prompt. #384 made the payload a schema, which
drops the field; it was unused before.

**Reproduced** by reading: `git grep -n surroundingEpisodes apps/web/lambda`
finds nothing.

### Proposed fix

Add `surroundingEpisodes` to the `season-outline` schema and pass it to the
outline prompt as context, or remove it from the action's input.
