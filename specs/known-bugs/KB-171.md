## KB-171 — Two figures called engagement leave shares out, unlike every other engagement rate

**Severity:** Low. The Top Shorts card's "% eng" and the studio overview's
"Avg. Engagement" read lower than the engagement rate every other card
shows for the same videos, because they divide likes and comments by
views and leave shares out. Nothing says so on screen. **Found:**
2026-10-02, building FILM-1732, whose sweep (FILM-1732 notes §2) listed the
Shorts read as escaping FILM-1713's one definition.

### What happens

FILM-1713 made `engagementRatio` — (likes + comments + shares) / views —
the one engagement definition, and its class guard
(`packages/clickhouse/__tests__/engagement-rate-one-definition.test.ts`)
looks for a likes + comments + shares sum. A likes + comments sum does not
match it, so two copies of a second definition survived:

- `getShortsSourcePerformance`, the Top Shorts card's `engagement`
  (`packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/language-analytics.ts:716`).
- The studio project overview's `avgEngagementRate`
  (`apps/web/app/home/[account]/studio/[projectSlug]/page.tsx:273`).

FILM-1732 kept both figures as they were — its rule is that no figure
changes — and moved them into one named function,
`recordedLikesAndCommentsPercent`
(`packages/clickhouse/src/lib/measures.ts:790`), so they are recorded and
cannot be recomputed inline (FILM-1732's class scan).

### Reproduced

`packages/clickhouse/__tests__/rate-denominator-records.test.ts`, "likes
and comments per view leaves shares out": 50 likes, 30 comments and 20
shares over 1,000 views give 8 through this figure and 10 through
`recordedEngagementRatePercent`.

### Proposed fix

Point both at `recordedEngagementRatePercent`, and delete
`recordedLikesAndCommentsPercent`. It changes two figures upward, so it
waits on the owner's 2026-09-22 ruling (FILM-1722 §12) like any other
figure change, or is decided with it.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Top Shorts card and the studio overview show the same engagement
      rate as the analytics dashboard for the same videos and window
- [ ] `recordedLikesAndCommentsPercent` is gone, and a unit test fails a
      likes + comments rate anywhere outside `lib/measures.ts`
