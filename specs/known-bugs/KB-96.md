## KB-96 — Account usage is never reset: nothing calls `reset_monthly_usage`

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #374: resolved by removal.** The owner decided
> to remove the budget machinery rather than fix it ("Remove the budget
> machinery"): the cap defaulted to NULL, nothing in the app could set it,
> and so no account was ever blocked. `reset_monthly_usage`, the usage
> counter and the cap are dropped; there is nothing left to reset. A
> repo-scan test (`apps/web/test/account-budget-retired.test.ts`) and
> pgTAP (`account-budget-retired.test.sql`) keep them from coming back.

**Severity:** Low today, High once a budget is set. **Found:** KB-83
(2026-09-24), by reading and search. **Open.**

`current_usage_cents` is documented as "Current month usage … Reset monthly"
(`apps/web/supabase/migrations/20251211090557_add-account-budget-tracking.sql:10`),
and `reset_monthly_usage()` exists (`:92`), but nothing calls it: no cron, no
route, no worker (`git grep reset_monthly_usage` finds only the migration).
Since KB-83, voice spend is counted, so usage now only grows. An account
whose owner sets a `monthly_budget_cents` will be refused voice generation
for good once a month's spend passes it.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A scheduled job resets usage at the start of each month, with a test that runs it
- [ ] Or usage is derived per month (from `generation_jobs.cost_cents`) instead of a counter
