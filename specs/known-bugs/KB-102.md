## KB-102 — Capability matrix says YouTube revenue access is authorised, a state it defines as verified against a live account; no live check has run

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #368** (FILM-1703 completion).
> `authorised` no longer means "seen working". It now says our OAuth config
> asks and no vendor review is known to block it. Every authorised entry
> carries exactly one of `verified: { on, how }` or
> `pendingVerification: { owner, question }`, enforced by the type
> (`AccessAxis` in `packages/clickhouse/src/lib/data-provenance.ts`). YouTube
> revenue is pending on FILM-1725 (Checks F and G). Fixing it found the same
> overclaim on three more surfaces: YouTube Analytics, YouTube Reporting and
> the Instagram user node. They are now pending on the owner, because rows in
> local and CI ClickHouse come from fixtures. No entry is `verified` yet.

**Severity:** Low — no surface reads the matrix yet, so no creator has seen
the claim. FILM-1705 and FILM-1726 would have rendered it: a creator could
be told revenue is available when Google may never grant the scope. **Found:**
completing FILM-1703.

**What happened.** `AccessState` documented `authorised` as "scope held AND
verified against a live account". FILM-1711 added
`yt-analytics-monetary.readonly` to the YouTube OAuth config, and the matrix
entry for `revenue × youtube` moved from `scope_missing` to `authorised`, with
a test pinning it (`data-provenance.test.ts`, "YouTube revenue is authorised
(FILM-1711) but still not ingested"). But:

- FILM-1725 Check F has not run: nobody knows whether Google's consent screen
  grants this sensitive scope to our app.
- FILM-1725 Check G has not run, and FILM-1711's own criterion "a real Partner
  Program channel's revenue_records holds non-zero `source = 'api'` rows" is
  `met: false`.
- `docs/platform-capability-reference.md` says the scope is "held only by
  connections made or re-consented after it". That is per connection, which
  the static matrix cannot know.

**Reproduced.** A structural rule requiring every `authorised` entry to carry
evidence or an owner, run against the unchanged matrix, failed on 12 entries:
every YouTube family on the Analytics and Reporting surfaces, YouTube revenue,
and Instagram `channel_totals`.

**Acceptance.** No entry says `authorised` without `verified` or
`pendingVerification` (type and runtime rule). No entry is `verified`
without a date and how. Mutation guards "YouTube revenue drops its pending
verification…" and "A surface claims a live verification nobody recorded"
(`tooling/mutation-guards/film-1703.json`) each go red.
