## KB-148 — No screen publishes to X: the publish screen never sends X a video

> **Fixed (2026-10-01), #TBD.** Owner decision: X takes full videos and
> Shorts from the episode publish screen. `FULL_VIDEO_PLATFORMS` and
> `SHORTS_PLATFORMS` in `@kit/publishing/lib/constants` (with `takesVideo()`)
> are now the one list the screen, its video cards, the upload dialog and
> scheduling read, and both include X. A publish X would refuse — no
> `media.write`, a video outside X's limits, a title over 280 characters —
> is refused as a value before anything is written (FILM-1729 §8).
> Proved in a production build against FILM-1802's X sandbox:
> `apps/e2e/tests/platform-connections/sandbox-x-upload-scope.spec.ts`
> (refused → reconnect → published). LinkedIn is still on no screen; that
> is not this bug's subject and is left for its own decision.

**Severity:** Medium. A team can connect X on the Platforms page, which
promises video uploads ("Video uploads to X need the media.write
permission…"), and then nothing in the product publishes to it. No data is
lost and nothing fails; the feature is just not reachable, which is why it is
not High. **Found:** 2026-10-01, building FILM-1729's Playwright flow (refused
→ reconnect → accepted), which had no screen to drive.

### Reproduced (2026-10-01)

- The episode publish screen builds its uploads in
  `buildPlatformConfigsAndConfirm`
  (`apps/web/app/home/[account]/studio/[projectSlug]/episodes/[episodeSlug]/publish/_components/publish-screen.tsx`).
  Full videos go only to connections whose platform is in
  `['youtube', 'facebook']`, and shorts only to
  `['youtube', 'instagram', 'facebook', 'tiktok']`. An X connection matches
  neither list, so `publishToAllAction` is never called for X from this
  screen.
- `PublishHub` (`packages/features/publishing/src/components/publish-hub.tsx`),
  whose platform selector lists `twitter`, is exported from `@kit/publishing`
  and imported by no route: `git grep -n "PublishHub" -- apps packages`
  matches only its own file, the barrel and a type comment.
- LinkedIn is in the same position: neither list includes `linkedin`.

So the only way an X publish happens is a `publishes` row with
`platform = 'twitter'` that the scheduled-publish cron picks up, and no screen
writes one.

### What follows from it

FILM-1729's server-side checks are built and unit-tested: the publish
action's refusal value, and the worker's reconnect text for a connection
without `media.write`. The criterion "Playwright spec covers: publish
refused → re-authorise → second publish accepted" cannot be met until a
screen sends X a video. It stays open with `closed_by: KB-148`.

### Proposed fix

A product decision (owner): which content goes to X — the full video, a
short, or both — and whether LinkedIn joins it. Then add the platform to the
matching list in `buildPlatformConfigsAndConfirm`, with the tweet-length
limit on the title that `metadata-editor.tsx` already knows (`isTweet`).
