## KB-124 — Deep Dive median of an even number of videos shows the upper middle value, not the median

> **Fixed (2026-09-30), #482.** The median, p25/p75 and cohort queries use
> `ifNotFinite(quantileExactInclusive(…), 0)`: the interpolating quantile
> averages the two middle values, and `ifNotFinite` keeps the empty case at 0
> (it answers `nan`, where `quantileExact` answered 0). `pnpm --filter
> @kit/clickhouse verify` pins even (2000), odd (2000) and empty (0) against
> ClickHouse 24.8, and the FILM-1706 evidence spec now expects 2.0K. The
> figures creators see change for any month with an even number of videos;
> the card's own wording was not changed.

**Severity:** Low. It affects every creator reading "Median views per video"
(and its p25/p75 band, and the upload-cohort medians) for a month with an
even number of videos: the figure is the upper of the two middle values, so
it is overstated. **Found:** FILM-1706's evidence run.

**Reproduced.** `apps/e2e/tests/analytics/card-shell-evidence.spec.ts`
seeds two YouTube videos uploaded in the same month, with 1,000 and 3,000
views. The median card reads **3.0K**. The median of 1,000 and 3,000 is
2,000.

**Why.** `quantileExact(0.5)` (`packages/clickhouse/src/queries-advanced.ts:312`
and `:331`, and `quantileExactIf` at `:698-700` for cohorts) returns the
element at index floor(0.5 × n) of the sorted values. For n = 2 that is the
second one. It is exact, as its comment at `:641` intends, but it is not the
median a reader computes.

**Proposed fix.** Use an interpolating exact quantile (`quantileExactInclusive`,
which interpolates linearly between the two middle values; check it on
ClickHouse 24.8 first), after deciding which median the product means. It changes figures creators already see, so it needs a
note on the card or in its details. The evidence spec's expectation (3.0K)
changes with it.

**Acceptance.** Seeded 1,000 and 3,000 reads 2.0K. A unit test in
`@kit/clickhouse` against the live check pins the even-count case.
