**LinkedIn's callback records the scopes it asked for as the grant** (found while verifying FILM-1711, 2026-09-25)
- `apps/web/app/api/platforms/callback/linkedin/route.ts:164-181` writes `LINKEDIN_OAUTH_CONFIG.scopes.company` or `.personal` into `platform_connections.scopes`, not what LinkedIn granted. That is the shape FILM-1711 (#289) removed from the YouTube, TikTok, X and Meta callbacks because it would mark a connection authorised for scopes it never received. LinkedIn has no analytics requirement in `packages/features/publishing/src/oauth/analytics-scopes.ts`, so `resolveAnalyticsAccess` returns `no_provider` and nothing reads the column for LinkedIn today; it becomes a defect the day a LinkedIn analytics provider or scope check is added. The fix is `parseGrantedScopes(tokens.scope)` as the other callbacks do, if LinkedIn's token response carries `scope` (not checked).
