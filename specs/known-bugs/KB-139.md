## KB-139 — Reads that find a project through seasons drop every episode in no season

> **Fixed (2026-09-28), #PR.** All five reads now use `episodes.project_id`,
> and `packages/shared/__tests__/kb139-project-through-episode.test.ts` fails
> any source that reaches a project through `seasons!inner` or
> `episodes.seasons.` (red on the unfixed code). The music timeline's
> Regenerate on a cue in an episode with no season now gets past the cue
> lookup (`music-timeline.spec.ts`, "KB-139"; red on the unfixed code,
> production build).

**Severity:** Medium. Nothing is lost, but whole episodes vanish from
analytics totals and reports without an error, and a paid generation cannot be
started for them. **Found:** 2026-09-28, building the reach page (#463), whose
first draft copied the account dashboard's join.

`episodes.season_id` is nullable (`on delete set null`), and seeded and
imported episodes are often in no season. KB-48 R9 fixed the same shape in an
RLS policy (audio cues found their project through seasons) but not in the
application reads, which had five:

| File | What an episode in no season did |
|---|---|
| `audio-generation/…/audio-cue-actions.ts` (`generateAudioForCue`) | The cue was "not found": PostgREST's "cannot coerce the result to a single JSON object" |
| `content-analytics/…/account-dashboard-actions.ts` | Its publishes were left out of the account dashboard's totals |
| `content-analytics/…/aggregation-queries.ts` (project audience) | Left out of the view weights behind the audience splits |
| `content-analytics/…/aggregation-queries.ts` (project publishes) | Left out of the project's publish list |
| `content-analytics/…/report-actions.ts` | Left out of generated reports |

### Reproduced (2026-09-28, `main` b6a66416, production build)

A seeded music episode with no season, the project's music model set: clicking
Regenerate on its cue answered with the lookup's PostgREST error. With the fix
the first answer comes from the queue step instead (in the ⚫️ Test job, which
has no LLM queue, its "LLM_JOBS_QUEUE_URL not configured").

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] No read finds a project through seasons (source scan, red first)
- [x] A cue on an episode in no season gets past its lookup, in a production build (red first)
