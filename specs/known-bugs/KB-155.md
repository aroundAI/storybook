## KB-155 — Deep Dive median chart draws its bars outside the chart: a percentage margin resolves against width

> **Fixed (2026-10-01), #TBD-1707.** Each band and the median line are placed
> absolutely by `bottom` inside a full-height bucket, so a 200-view bucket in a
> chart whose tallest value is 200 sits at the top of the chart, not above it.
> `__tests__/six-tab-adoption.test.tsx` ("the median chart draws inside
> itself") fails with the margin put back.

**Severity:** Low. The figures, the claim and the bar titles were right; the
chart under "Median views per video" looked empty, because every bar was
drawn far above it and clipped or overlapping the cards above. Nothing was
miscounted. **Found:** 2026-10-01, building FILM-1707: the dashed rule it
draws on a bucket crossing a view-definition change made the bucket's real
box visible — a line reaching up from the card into the page header.

`components/deep-dive/median-views-card.tsx` gave the band
`marginBottom: (p25 / max) * 100%`. A vertical margin's percentage is of the
containing block's **width** (CSS 2.1 §8.3), so for a single-video bucket
(p25 = max) the margin was the full card width, ~500px, inside a 96px-tall
flex row aligned to the end: the bucket grew upward by that much. FILM-1705's
own evidence screenshot shows the same card with no visible bars.

### Acceptance

- [x] No element of the chart is positioned by a percentage vertical margin,
  and a band's `bottom` is its p25 as a share of the chart's height
  (six-tab-adoption.test.tsx, red with the old card).
