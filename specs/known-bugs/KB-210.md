## KB-210 — Long videos scheduled over MCP posted with no thumbnail, though the episode had one

> **Fixed (2026-10-19), #689.** One resolver,
> `packages/features/publishing/src/lib/episode-thumbnail.ts`, answers "the
> thumbnail for this episode's long video in language X": the
> `episode_thumbnails` row in that language, else the default, else none.
> `preparePublish` uses it when a long-video target names no thumbnail, so
> MCP `schedule_publish`, `schedule_release` (which had its own copy) and the
> Publish screen's publish and schedule actions all store one. The publish
> worker uses it for a long-video job that carries none
> (`apps/web/lambda/publish-worker/job-thumbnail.ts`), which is what fixes
> the rows already scheduled: the job is built from the row, and the row has
> none. A Short never gets one (KB-197). Every URL still goes through
> `ownedEpisodeThumbnail` (KB-104). The in-process cron route
> (`processScheduledPublishes`, used by the vendor-sandbox specs) uses it
> too. A thumbnail YouTube refuses
> (`thumbnails.set`) or Facebook cannot be sent (`thumb`) leaves the video
> up and is recorded on the row as `metadata.thumbnailError`. Tests, red on
> main: `packages/features/publishing/__tests__/schedule-publishes.test.ts`
> ("gives a long video with no thumbnail the episode's…"),
> `publish-thumbnail-ownership.test.ts` (the Publish screen's action),
> `apps/web/lambda/publish-worker/__tests__/kb210-thumbnail.test.ts`,
> `kb210-youtube-thumbnail.test.ts` and `meta-handlers.test.ts`.

**Severity:** High. Every long video on the owner's two projects went out
without its thumbnail: the 4 already published on YouTube and Facebook, and
the 11 still scheduled would have. The owner set them by hand in YouTube
Studio and on Facebook.

**Found:** 2026-10-19, production: every long-video `publishes` row of both
projects had `thumbnail_url = NULL`, while each episode had an
`episode_thumbnails` row (language `en`, `is_default` true). The rows were
written by MCP `schedule_publish` (`metadata.createdVia` set, `slot_at`
null).

**Cause:** thumbnails are stored in `episode_thumbnails`, one per language,
by the Publish screen's upload and MCP `finalize_episode_thumbnail` alike
(`saveEpisodeThumbnail`). The schedule services took
`target.thumbnailUrl ?? null` and fell back only to `episodes.thumbnail_url`,
a legacy column nothing writes any more, so a caller that named no thumbnail
stored none. `schedule_release` had its own lookup of `episode_thumbnails`
and was right; `schedule_publish` had none. The scheduled Lambda builds the
job from the row, so the worker sent nothing, and the YouTube and Facebook
uploads, which do set a thumbnail when given one, had nothing to set.

**Production rows:** nothing is changed in the database. The 11 scheduled
rows keep `thumbnail_url = NULL`; the worker looks up the episode's
thumbnail when each one posts. The 4 published videos already have the
thumbnails the owner set by hand.
