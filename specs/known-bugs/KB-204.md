## KB-204 — Publish All posted every Short of an episode at once, past the channels' posting rules

> **Fixed (2026-10-19), #685.** The Publish screen's button is now
> **Publish video now**: it posts the long video(s) now and sends every
> Shorts group through the cadence release (`scheduleCadenceRelease`, the
> path Schedule Release uses), so each Short gets a `slot_at` at the end of
> its channel's Shorts queue, within the channel's Shorts a day, waiting for
> its long video. The confirm step lists what posts now and when each Short
> is queued (previewed with the long video going now), and nothing already
> on a channel (out or on its way) is offered again; the list is read afresh
> on every press, because the one on screen lagged a press made right after
> the last (the Playwright spec caught it offering the same three Shorts
> again). A long video that fails leaves its Shorts unqueued. No Short
> carries a thumbnail from the screen.
>
> The class fix is server-side: `cadenceRefusal`
> (`packages/features/publishing/src/server/cadence-guard.service.ts`, over
> the pure `cadenceConflicts`) holds every post whose time the caller chose
> — `publishToAllAction` (publish now and staggered schedules) and
> `schedulePublishes` (MCP `schedule_publish`) — to the channel's rules: a
> Short on a channel-local day that already has as many Shorts as the
> channel's Short times, or a long video closer than `minDaysBetween` local
> days to another on the channel, is refused as a value, unless
> `ignoreCadence: true`. It reads the channel through `channel-calendar.ts`,
> the reader the release planner now shares, so the two count a day alike.
> The MCP tool takes `ignoreCadence`, and its guide says to use
> `schedule_release` for an episode.
>
> Tests: `packages/features/publishing/__tests__/cadence-guard.test.ts` and
> the KB-204 cases in `publish-actions.test.ts` and
> `schedule-publishes.test.ts`: with the guard neutralised, 7 fail on their
> assertions. `apps/e2e/tests/publishing/publish-now-queues-shorts.spec.ts`
> drives the button against the local database.
>
> Not covered here: `apps/e2e/tests/publishing/sandbox-ai-label.spec.ts`
> (sandbox-only, not in CI) published a Short twice through Publish All to
> reach Instagram; it now needs the scheduled path to send the Short.

**Severity:** High. Spam risk on every channel of the project: YouTube
refuses further uploads for the day, and a burst of Reels on Facebook and
Instagram is the pattern the platforms penalise.

**Found:** 2026-10-19, in production. Publish All, pressed twice on one
episode, posted 9 YouTube Shorts, 7 Facebook Reels and 7 Instagram Reels in
nine minutes, until YouTube answered "exceeded the number of videos they may
upload". Separately, a producer session scheduled three long videos 30
minutes apart through MCP `schedule_publish`.

**Cause:** `handlePublish` → `buildPlatformConfigsAndConfirm` →
`confirmAndUpload` in `publish-screen.tsx` built one publish-now config for
the long video and one for every Shorts group × Shorts channel, and
`publishToAllAction` sent each at once. Neither it nor `schedulePublishes`
read `channel_posting_cadences`: only the release planner and the scheduled
job's gate held posts to them, and a publish-now post never meets the gate.
The Shorts configs also carried `getThumbnailForLanguage(lang)` (the server
has dropped it since KB-197).
