## KB-150 — A failed analytics sync is shown nowhere: getSyncStatusAction has no caller

> **Fixed (2026-10-01), #TBD.** The episode analytics page shows, per synced
> platform, when its figures were last refreshed (or "Not synced yet", never a
> made-up time) and, when the latest sync failed, why: the product's sentence
> for the status (rate-limited, access refused, failed; or a disconnected or
> unauthorised channel), the platform's own reason, and whether it is retried
> or paused until a reconnect. The Video Log flags the row "Sync failed". Both
> read `getSyncStatusAction`, now the one reader of `publishes.metadata.sync`
> (by episode or by publish ids, paged, a failed read returned as a refusal);
> its schedule verdict is `syncEligibility` itself. Found while fixing: the
> sync stored this app's paraphrase rather than TikTok's reason, and a TikTok
> 5xx as the raw JSON body; the provider now keeps TikTok's `code: message`
> and `classifySyncFailure` records it. `sandbox-sync-failure.spec.ts` drives
> 429, 401 and 503 through the sandbox, asserts each message and that the
> next successful sync clears it; it goes red with the fix reverted.

**Severity:** Medium — when a vendor rate-limits, refuses a scope or fails a
sync, the creator's figures simply stop moving and nothing says why. **Found:**
FILM-1804 §2 "Vendor errors": a rate limit and a 5xx on the analytics sync
have no message to assert.

The sync records each failure on the publish
(`publishes.metadata.sync.last_sync_status` = `rate_limited`, `scope_error`
or `failed`, with `last_error` and `consecutive_failures`;
packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/analytics-sync-cron.ts). The
only reader is `getSyncStatusAction`
(packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/sync-actions.ts), exported
from the package's server index, and no component imports it: there is no
data-test, badge or message for any of these states. After five consecutive
failures the publish stops being synced at all, also silently.

**Reproduced** (`apps/e2e/tests/analytics/sandbox-sync.spec.ts`, "TikTok
rate-limits the sync"): with the sandbox's `POST /__sandbox/fail` returning
429 on TikTok's `/v2/video/query/`, the sync records `rate_limited` on the
publish, and nothing in the app reads it — `getSyncStatusAction` is
imported by no component (searched across `apps/web` and `packages`).

**Fix:** surface the sync state where the figures are read (the episode
analytics page and the Video Log): when the last sync failed, say when the
figures were last refreshed and why the latest attempt failed, in the
product's words per status.

**Acceptance:** a sandbox flow that fails the sync with 429, 401 and 5xx
shows the matching message, and a later successful sync clears it.
