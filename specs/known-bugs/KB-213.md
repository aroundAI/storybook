## KB-213 — The same Short was posted twice to one Facebook Page, and a Page Meta had blocked kept being sent posts

> **Fixed (2026-10-20), #692.** Three changes.
>
> **No second post.** `duplicatePostRefusal`
> (`packages/features/publishing/src/lib/duplicate-post.ts`) is the one
> rule: another post of the same episode, kind (`full` / `short`) and
> connection that is `published` in the last 30 days, or `publishing` and
> started in the last 30 minutes, with the same title or the same file (a
> Short's Shorts group in the same language; for a long video the episode's
> video in the same language). A `deleted` row does not count, so a video
> taken down on the platform can be posted again once marked removed. The
> publish worker asks it after marking the row `publishing`
> (`duplicateJobRefusal` in `apps/web/lambda/publish-worker/job-guard.ts`),
> so of two copies in one SQS batch exactly one goes; publish now, retry
> and the scheduled job all reach a platform through the worker (KB-206).
> The scheduled job's in-app path (`processScheduledPublishes`), the
> Publish screen (`publishToAllAction`) and every schedule path
> (`prepareScheduledRows`: MCP `schedule_publish`, `schedule_release`,
> breaking releases) ask it too, before a row is written. The refusal reads
> "Already posted to this account as <url> on <date>; not posted again."
> `ignoreCadence` does not override it.
>
> **Hand-marked removals are asked about again.** The hourly analytics sync
> (`reprobeRemovedPublishes`,
> `packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/removed-reprobe.ts`) asks
> the platform, once a day, about each `deleted` YouTube, Facebook or
> Instagram row published in the last 14 days whose removal was not KB-207's
> own not-found: a "Mark as removed", or a row set `deleted` directly in the
> database. Rows of deleted episodes are included. A definite 200 with the
> id (Meta `GET /{id}?fields=id`; YouTube `videos.list` listing it) sets the
> row back to `published` with `metadata.restoredNote` "still live on
> <platform> at <ts>; it had been marked removed"; a definite not-found
> records `metadata.reprobedAt`; a token, permission, rate-limit or network
> failure changes nothing. The Schedule tab's "Deleted episodes (N hidden)"
> now also says how many of them are still live.
>
> **Meta code 368 pauses that connection.** A Facebook or Instagram upload
> that fails with code 368 (any subcode) writes
> `platform_connections.metadata.publishBlock` (Meta's message, code,
> subcode and time) on that connection only, and the row fails with "Not
> posted: Facebook asked you to confirm your identity for this Page. Open
> the Facebook app and follow its instructions, then press Retry on
> Settings → Platforms…", acknowledged rather than retried. While it is
> set, the scheduled job leaves that connection's due posts `scheduled`
> (counted as `paused`), and the worker puts a job for it back to
> `scheduled` without sending it. Settings → Platforms shows the banner
> "Facebook asked you to confirm your identity for this Page. Open the
> Facebook app, follow its instructions, then press Retry." with Meta's own
> words; Retry (`retryPublishingAction`) and a manual token refresh clear
> it, and reconnecting writes a fresh metadata without it.
>
> Tests, each red without its fix:
> `apps/web/lambda/publish-worker/__tests__/kb213-duplicates-and-blocks.test.ts`
> (the real worker and job guard on an in-memory database: 6 of 9 fail on
> `main`'s worker: the duplicate Short, the same group under another
> title, the second long video, two copies in one batch, the 368 block
> recorded, later posts held),
> `apps/web/lambda/scheduled-publish/__tests__/index.test.ts` ("leaves a
> paused connection's posts scheduled and sends the others", red on
> `main`'s job),
> `packages/features/publishing/__tests__/schedule-publishes.test.ts`
> ("schedules nothing that is already posted to the same channel, and
> allows another channel") and
> `packages/features/publishing/__tests__/publish-now-shorts-group.test.ts`
> ("Publish Now refuses a Short the channel already has"), both red
> without the schedule-time check,
> `packages/features/content-analytics/__tests__/removed-reprobe.test.ts`
> (a live reel published again, a 404 and a failed call leave it removed),
> `packages/features/publishing/__tests__/retry-publishing-action.test.ts`
> and the banner's
> `apps/web/app/home/[account]/studio/[projectSlug]/platforms/__tests__/publish-block-notice.test.tsx`.

**Severity:** High. A duplicate Reel on the owner's Page cut its reach, and
the block it led to stopped all posting to the Page; the scheduler would
have kept posting into the block, which is how a temporary block becomes a
longer one.

**Found:** 2026-10-20, production. Facts as the owner and the logs gave
them:

- On 19 Oct 2026, 02:34–02:41 IST, "Publish All" ran twice (KB-204, since
  fixed) and posted 7 Fault & Frontier Shorts to Facebook, among them the
  same "Would you send her through?" twice: Reels 1686907816188751 and
  1875259416809672.
- The duplicate rows were later marked `deleted` by hand (the KB-207
  clean-up) on the owner's word, but at least some stayed live. The Page
  shows about 11 FF-001 Reels.
- On 20 Oct 2026 at 00:31 UTC Meta refused the next post with
  OAuthException code 368, subcode 4854002: "Confirm your identity before
  you can publish as this Page." That is an integrity block on the Page,
  and the scheduler would have kept trying it.
- Ledger Autopsy was not affected: its burst had no Facebook Shorts.

### What happened

- Nothing on any publishing path asked whether the account already had
  the video. KB-204 stopped the double click; a second click, a retry or a
  second schedule of the same Short would still have posted it again.
- A row marked `deleted` is left out of everything (the posting rules, the
  reports), and nothing ever asked the platform again, so a removal written
  by hand that was wrong stayed wrong.
- The worker read a refused upload as an ordinary failure. Nothing knew
  that Meta's code 368 means "stop posting to this Page", so every later
  post to it would have been sent and refused.

### Not covered

- Code 368 is read from the error text the Facebook and Instagram
  providers throw (`"code":368`, `(#368)` or Meta's "Confirm your identity
  before you can publish" wording). A provider path that throws only a
  generic message would not be recognised.
- A schedule is refused only against posts that are `published` or
  `publishing`; two `scheduled` copies of one Short on one channel can
  both be written, and the worker then sends only the first.
- The existing extra FF-001 Reels on the Page are not removed: which to
  take down is the owner's call, on Facebook. The re-probe marks the live
  ones `published` again, so the guard counts them.
- Screenshot of the Settings → Platforms banner: pending; the local stack
  was not up, so the banner is covered by a React Testing Library test.

### Acceptance

- [x] A duplicate Short on the same connection is refused at upload and at
  scheduling, with where it already is, and allowed on another connection.
- [x] A long video of an episode already published on the connection is
  refused.
- [x] A hand-marked removal still live on its platform is published again
  with a note; a not-found or a failed call leaves it.
- [x] A code 368 pauses that connection only: its posts stay scheduled and
  are not sent, others carry on, and Retry clears it.
- [x] Settings → Platforms says what to do, in plain words.
