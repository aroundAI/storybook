## KB-153 — A Facebook video reads as 0 views in per-video lists and the platform split

**Severity:** Low today, Medium once Facebook is switched on. No Facebook
row exists anywhere until the owner names `facebook` in
`ANALYTICS_SCOPES_ENABLED` (FILM-1720 ships dark), so nobody sees it yet.
Once rows exist, a Facebook video would show "0 views" in the video log and
the content table, and Facebook would appear as a 0-view slice in the
overview's platform split: a measurement of nothing, where the truth is that
Facebook has no single view. **Found:** 2026-10-01, building FILM-1720.

### What happens

Migration 020 makes `video_metrics.views` Nullable and every Facebook row
writes NULL there: Facebook counts four kinds of view and FILM-1722 names
none of them "views". ClickHouse sums a column of NULLs to NULL, and three
readers turn that into `Number(null)`, which is 0:

| Reader | Where the 0 shows |
|---|---|
| `queryPerVideoTotals` (`packages/clickhouse/src/queries.ts`) | video log, content table, scheduled reports |
| `queryVideoViewsAtAge` (`queries-advanced.ts`) | video log's views-at-age columns and its sort |
| `queryPlatformBreakdown` (`queries.ts`) | overview platform split, account dashboard |

Reproduced against a throwaway ClickHouse 24.8 with migration 020 applied:
one Facebook row (`views` NULL, 900 first plays, 5 likes) read back as

```json
{ "perVideoTotalsViews": 0, "viewsAtAgeLifetime": 0,
  "platformBreakdown": [{ "platform": "facebook", "views": 0, "likes": 5 }] }
```

Not affected: pooled totals (`sum` skips NULL, so Facebook adds nothing, as
intended), the reach page (fixed in FILM-1720 with `measuredViews`), and the
median, cohort and segment readers (FILM-1720 keeps them to
`VIEWS_COLUMN_PLATFORMS`, which `verify-queries.ts` proves by hand).

### Proposed fix

The FILM-1720 reach fix, once, at the readers: views become `number | null`
on `PerVideoTotals`, `VideoAgeRow` and `PlatformBreakdown` for a platform
outside `VIEWS_COLUMN_PLATFORMS`, and each surface renders "Not measured"
with `NO_SINGLE_VIEW_REASON`. The `measured` flags (`MeasuredColumns`, KB-114)
are the precedent for the per-video totals.

### Acceptance criteria

- A Facebook video in the video log shows "Not measured" for views, never 0,
  and sorts after every measured video.
- The platform split names Facebook without a views figure.
- `verify-queries.ts` asserts each of the three readers against a
  NULL-views Facebook row.
