## KB-120 — Shot generation ignores the shot duration and video provider the user chose

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #405.** The `shot-generation` job carries
> `shotDurationMin`/`shotDurationMax` (3–10 s, default 5–8, max ≥ min; one
> definition, `SHOT_DURATION_LIMITS` in `@kit/prompt-engine/llm-job-payloads`,
> used by the action's schema too). The handler hands the range to the Shot
> Orchestrator; the Shot Director passes it to `scene-shot-generation` (v4,
> which said "MAX 8 seconds") and clamps every returned shot to it.
>
> **What the user sees:** nothing new. There is no control for the range; the
> one caller (`screenplay-screen.tsx`) sends 5–8, and shots now land in 5–8 s
> instead of "at most 8". **`videoProvider` is removed**, not wired up: in-app
> video generation is retired, the value fed nothing, and no control showed it
> (the caller hard-coded `veo-3.1`). Red on the base branch:
> `episodes/__tests__/shot-duration.test.ts` 4 of 4, plus the producer and
> handler cases (guards SD1–SD4).

**Severity:** Low — the choices are silently unused, nothing is corrupted.
**Found:** KB-33's producer audit (worker-authz, #384).

`generateShotListAction`
(`packages/features/episodes/src/lib/server/mutations/shot-list-actions.ts`)
validates `shotDurationMin`, `shotDurationMax` and `videoProvider`
(`packages/features/episodes/src/lib/schemas/shot-list.schema.ts`) and sent
them in the `shot-generation` job. The worker's handler
(`apps/web/lambda/llm-worker/handlers/shot-generation.ts`) never read them:
its payload type had no such fields. #384 made the payload a schema, which
drops them at the producer; the user's choice was unused before and is now
not sent.

**Reproduced** by reading both sides against the schema: `git grep -n
"shotDuration\|videoProvider" apps/web/lambda` finds nothing.

### Proposed fix

Either pass them into the shot-generation prompt (a schema field and a
handler change), or remove the controls that set them.
