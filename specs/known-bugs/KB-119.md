## KB-119 — The Delete All dialog says platform videos must be deleted by hand, and then deletes them on YouTube and Facebook

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #392.** The dialog is titled "Delete All
> Published Videos" and says it deletes the episode's videos on YouTube and
> Facebook and removes every publish record, and that videos on TikTok,
> Instagram, X and LinkedIn stay up. The platform names come from
> `PLATFORMS_DELETED_ON_UNPUBLISH` (`@kit/publishing/lib/platforms`), and
> `unpublish-platforms.test.ts` fails if that list and the publish worker's
> delete handlers differ. Screenshot and E2E: `delete-all-copy.spec.ts`.

**Severity:** Medium — the copy invites an irreversible action by saying it
is harmless: a creator reading "this only removes records from our system"
takes live videos down. **Found:** worker-authz, KB-47's survey of the publish
queue (#384).

`delete-all-dialog.tsx` said: "Note: This only removes records from our
system. Videos already published to platforms will need to be deleted
manually." Confirming it calls `deleteEpisodePublishesAction`, which queues a
delete job per publish; the publish worker's `deleteFromPlatform` deletes the
video on YouTube and Facebook (`deleteFromYouTube`, `deleteFromFacebook`) and
skips the others.

**Reproduced:** red on `main` in `apps/e2e/tests/publishing/delete-all-copy.spec.ts`
(the dialog does not say what it deletes).

- [x] The dialog names the platforms it deletes on and the ones it does not
- [x] The names come from one list that is checked against the worker's delete handlers
