## KB-74 — Report files in the `reports` bucket are never deleted

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #362.** Owner decision (2026-09-25, following
> KB-20): report files are **kept until the account asks, and deleting the
> account is asking**; the owner chose automatic deletion on account delete.
> The hourly scheduled-reports cron now removes the report folder of every
> account that no longer exists — whichever way it was deleted — and legacy
> `scheduled/<reportId>/` folders whose schedule is gone (their only link to
> an account; the owner was told this before deciding). It deletes nothing
> if it cannot read which accounts exist. New scheduled reports are filed
> under their account, `exports/<accountId>/scheduled/<reportId>/…`, so they
> stay traceable after the schedule is deleted; the RLS path function
> already reads that shape. The data-deletion runbook gains the check and a
> "delete my reports" procedure, the privacy retention table a "Report Files
> — Until you ask" row, and `/data-deletion` §3 says account deletion
> removes them within 7 days. No time-based retention: a live account's
> files are never removed by age.
