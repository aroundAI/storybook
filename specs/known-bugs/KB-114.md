## KB-114 — TikTok stores 0 for subscribers gained, watch time and saves it never measures

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #PRNUM.** ClickHouse migration
> `014_unmeasured_counters_null` makes `saves`, `watch_time_seconds` and
> `subscribers_gained` Nullable, recreates `video_daily_stats`, and NULLs
> TikTok's three, Instagram's watch time and **YouTube's saves** (YouTube has
> no saves metric; the same rule, so it is in the same migration). The
> writers' types pin those to `null`. Per-video and daily reads return
> `measured` flags (`count(col) > 0`), and the reports use them: a CSV cell
> is blank with a fixed "(blank = not measured by the platform)" header
> note, the PDF prints "—" with a legend, and totals cover only the videos
> that measure the figure, "from N of M videos" (decision #33, A). The report
> summary is now one function instead of three copies. Its PDF cards had
> printed CTR, average duration and retention as `0` always; they now show
> the real figure or "—".
>
> **The per-connection subscriber series**, decided 2026-09-25 (option a):
> for a channel whose platform reports no daily gains and losses (TikTok,
> Instagram), the series draws straight lines between the follower counts
> actually recorded, labelled "reconstructed from follower snapshots". Each
> real snapshot is a point; the lines between are lighter and dotted, with no
> solid stroke; days between are never presented as measured movement, in
> the tooltip or elsewhere. `querySubscriberDeltas` now says whether a day's
> movement was measured, and an unmeasured day is neither movement nor
> evidence. No export carries subscriber levels, so none needed the label.

**Severity:** Medium — the same breach of "cannot measure is not measured
badly" as KB-111, on three more columns. **Found:** KB-111's scope decision,
2026-09-25. The zeros were read in the code; the readers below were checked.
The figures have not been reproduced on a page.

`normalizeAnalyticsData` (`packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/analytics-sync-cron.ts`)
writes literal zeros for TikTok where TikTok reports nothing on any surface we
can authorise:

- `saves: 0`, `watch_time_seconds: 0`. The code comment already says "structurally zero, not
  measured as zero" (saves: no creator-auth surface; watch time: Business API only);
- `subscribers_gained: 0` ("TikTok doesn't provide per-video follower gains").

Instagram has the same shape for `watch_time_seconds: 0` ("Not measured as
zero … we never request it", FILM-1712).

These go into `video_snapshots` and, through `computeSnapshotDelta`, into
`video_metrics` as a delta of 0. Unlike KB-111's columns they are **summed**, and
a 0 adds the same as a NULL, so most totals come out right. They are wrong where
the figure is shown per video or per platform, or used as a denominator:

- per-platform watch time (`queries.ts` has ~15 `sum(watch_time_seconds)` readers
  grouped by platform), where TikTok shows "0 hours watched";
- the per-connection subscriber series. `querySubscriberDeltas` reads TikTok's
  gained as 0. Since KB-111 it also reads a TikTok or Instagram loss as
  `ifNull(sum(lost), 0)`, which keeps the series as it was rather than
  dropping the day. So an Instagram "net" is gains, and a TikTok net is 0. Both
  are "not measured" dressed as a movement.

**Proposed fix:** the KB-111 pattern. Make the columns `Nullable` without a
default (recreating `video_daily_stats` first, then NULLing existing TikTok/IG
rows). Have the writers set `null`, and give the per-platform readers the
"from N of M videos" coverage label. The subscriber series needs a product
answer first: should a channel with no measured losses show a reconstructed
curve between follower snapshots, or none?

**Acceptance:** a TikTok video's watch time, saves and follower gains read "not
measured" on every surface that shows them per video or per platform. The
subscriber series for an Instagram connection no longer reports gains as net.
Each is asserted in `verify-queries.ts` and seen red first.
