## KB-126 — The season outliner sends characters, locations and recurring elements under names its prompt does not read

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #413.** The class, not just the outliner:
> `prompt-engine/__tests__/prompt-variables.test.ts` finds every call in the
> repo that names a template and passes a literal `variables` object (39),
> and fails on a placeholder the call leaves unfilled or a key the template
> does not declare; it also fails on any template placeholder that is not
> declared. On the base branch it failed **7 calls**:
>
> | Caller | Sent, and never read | Fix |
> |---|---|---|
> | Season Outliner | `existing_characters`, `existing_locations`, `recurring_element`; no verified facts | sent under the template's names; `recurring_element` added to `season-outline` (v3); facts from the job context |
> | Story Director | `style` (a dead constant); `viral_goals`; no verified facts | `style` dropped; `viral_goals` added to `story-generation` (v3); facts from the job context |
> | Ideation Director | `style`; `weak_indices` (so "regenerate the weak ideas" rewrote all of them) | `style` dropped; `weak_indices` added to `story-ideation` (v3), worded |
> | Screenplay Director | `genre`, `target_audience` | added to `screenplay-conversion` (v3) |
> | `agent-story-generation` | `style` | dropped; the other optional fields sent empty |
> | `extractDescriptionAction` | `role`, `arc` — and not `type_instructions`, which the template **requires** | one shared call with the batch path |
> | Season Analysis (Lambda) | nothing for `recurring_element` | the project's recurring elements |
>
> **What users see:** the sidebar's "extract description" for a character or
> location **always came back empty** — every call threw on the missing
> required variable, was caught, and returned `''`. It now returns the
> description. Everything else changes what the models are told, not the UI.

**Severity:** Medium — every season outline was written without the
project's characters, locations or recurring elements, and the model was
sent the literal placeholder text. **Found:** worker-authz, fixing KB-121
(#405).

`generateSeasonOutline` (`packages/features/episodes/src/agent/skills/season-outliner-skill.ts`)
called the `season-outline` prompt with `existing_characters`,
`existing_locations` and `recurring_element`. The template's variables are
`characters`, `locations` and `verified_facts`, and it had no recurring-element
variable. The prompt-engine renderer replaces only the names it is given, so
the template's placeholders were left as they were. The Lambda renderer
(`llm-worker/llm-utils.ts`) blanks an unfilled placeholder instead — the two
differ, and either way the data is missing, so the test reads the template
text rather than either renderer.

**Reproduced** by rendering the template with the skill's keys
(`loadAndRenderPrompt('season-outline', …)`, canary values in each): none of
the three canaries was in the prompt, and it read
`**Characters**:\n{{characters}}\n\n**Locations**:\n{{locations}}` verbatim.
`prompt-variables-callers.test.ts` renders what each fixed caller now sends:
4 of 4 red on the base branch.
