## KB-152 — A video's checkpoint maturity is judged on elapsed time in TypeScript and on calendar days in ClickHouse, so the Video Log and the cohort disagree near a boundary

> **Fixed (2026-10-01), #TBD.** `computeMaturity` and `computeIngestLagDays`
> now count UTC calendar days (`calendarDaysBetween`, `lib/video-age.ts`), the
> unit `dateDiff('day', …)` uses in `queryCohortMedians`. Found building
> FILM-1715, whose benchmark joins the two sides and would otherwise compare a
> video against a peer set built on different eligibility.

**Severity:** Low — for up to a day either side of a checkpoint, a video
uploaded late in the (UTC) day showed "not yet" for a figure the cohort
medians already counted, and an ingest that began the next calendar day read
as no lag at all. No figure was wrong; one side withheld it for a day longer.
**Found:** building FILM-1715 (self-benchmarking).

`queryCohortMedians` admits a video to "@Nd" when
`dateDiff('day', published_at, asOf) >= N` and sums metric dates whose
`dateDiff` from publication is `< N`. ClickHouse's `dateDiff('day')` counts
calendar-day boundaries. `computeMaturity` (Video Log, and now the benchmark's
subject side) floored elapsed milliseconds instead.

### Reproduced (2026-10-01, ClickHouse 24.8)

```sql
SELECT dateDiff('day', toDateTime('2026-01-01 23:00:00'), toDateTime('2026-01-31 01:00:00')),  -- 30
       dateDiff('day', toDateTime('2026-01-01 23:00:00'), toDateTime(toDate('2026-01-02')))     -- 1
```

`computeMaturity('2026-01-01 23:00:00', [30], 2026-01-31T01:00Z)[30]` returned
`false` (29 elapsed days) and `computeIngestLagDays('2026-01-01 23:00:00',
'2026-01-02')` returned `0`. The two new cases in
`packages/clickhouse/__tests__/video-age.test.ts` ("calendar days, as the
cohort query counts them") failed with exactly those values before the fix.
