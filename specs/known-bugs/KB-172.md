## KB-172 — Pooled rates count Facebook's likes and revenue over views that leave Facebook out

**Severity:** Medium. Any engagement rate or RPM that pools Facebook with
another platform reads high: Facebook's likes, comments, shares and
revenue are in the numerator, and its views — stored NULL, FILM-1720
option A — are not in the denominator. Since FILM-1732 the record beside
the figure says "Facebook is not in the denominator" while the figure
still counts Facebook's numerator, so the card contradicts itself.
**Found:** 2026-10-02, writing FILM-1732's read-level tests.

### What happens

The reads sum views with `addViews` / `viewsToAdd`, where Facebook adds
nothing, and sum every numerator in full:

- Episode totals: `totalLikes += stats.likes` for every publish
  (`packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/aggregation-queries.ts:317`),
  the views beside it through `addViews`.
- Language performance, the platform × language matrix and the format
  families: `current.likes += stats.likes`
  (`packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/language-analytics.ts:330`,
  `:470`), `target.likes += stats.likes` (`:590`).
- The account dashboard's total, from `queryTotals`, which sums Facebook's
  interactions with everyone's.
- The revenue summary's RPM: every revenue row is in the total, and the
  views are `viewsToAdd`
  (`packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/revenue-actions.ts:110`).
- Top content by revenue gives a Facebook item `views: 0` and `rpm: 0`
  (`packages/features/content-analytics/src/lib/revenue-by-currency.ts:425`)
  — a measured zero, where KB-153 makes every other read say "not
  measured".

### Reproduced

`packages/features/content-analytics/__tests__/rate-denominator-aggregation-reads.test.ts`:
an episode with a YouTube publish (1,000 views, 100 interactions) and a
Facebook one (NULL views, 10 interactions) reads 11%, not YouTube's 10%.
`rate-denominator-revenue-reads.test.ts`: Facebook's 400 cents lift the
summary RPM from 340 to 420. Both tests pin today's figure, as FILM-1732
requires.

### Proposed fix

Leave a platform's numerator out of any rate whose denominator leaves its
views out — the record already says which platforms those are
(`DenominatorStamp.platforms[].inDenominator`) — and give a Facebook item
in top content no RPM rather than 0. Every affected figure moves, so it
waits on the owner's 2026-09-22 ruling (FILM-1722 §12).

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A pooled engagement rate and RPM count only the numerators of
      platforms in the denominator, tested with a YouTube + Facebook fixture
- [ ] Top content by revenue shows a Facebook item's RPM as not measured
