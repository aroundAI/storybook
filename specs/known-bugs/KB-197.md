## KB-197 — A Short or Reel goes out with the long video's thumbnail

> **Fixed (2026-10-17), #668.** `publishThumbnail(contentType, own,
> episodeThumbnail)` in `packages/features/publishing/src/lib/publish-thumbnail.ts`
> gives a long video its own thumbnail, else the episode's, and a Short
> none. It is used where a publish is written (`schedulePublishes`, which
> cadence releases go through; Publish Now's insert) and where one is sent
> (Publish Now's upload, the retry, the scheduled Lambda, the in-process
> job), so a Short scheduled before the fix with the thumbnail on its row
> goes out without it. Tests: `schedule-publishes.test.ts` 'gives the long
> video the episode thumbnail and a Short none', `publish-now-shorts-group.test.ts`
> 'writes and uploads the short without the episode thumbnail', the
> scheduled Lambda's 'sends a Short no thumbnail'. Guards K1–K3 in
> `tooling/mutation-guards/film-2208-mcp-cadence-release.json`. Found while
> planning FILM-2208.

**Severity:** Medium. The owner posts four to seven Shorts per episode on
YouTube and Facebook. A 16:9 thumbnail cut for the long video, on a
vertical Short, shows wherever the platform uses the custom thumbnail.

### What happened

`schedulePublishes` and Publish Now wrote
`thumbnail_url: platform.thumbnailUrl ?? episodeThumbnail` for every
target. The scheduled Lambda sent
`publish.thumbnail_url || publish.episodes?.thumbnail_url`, and the
in-process job and the retry did the same through `ownedEpisodeThumbnail`.
None looked at `content_type`. The YouTube worker calls `thumbnails.set` on
whatever it is given, and the Facebook provider attaches it to the upload.

The Schedule Release panel already sent Shorts `thumbnailUrl: undefined`
("Shorts don't use custom thumbnails"); the server put the episode's back.
Publish Now sent the episode's thumbnail for Shorts outright.

### Reproduced

`schedule-publishes.test.ts` with an episode thumbnail: the YouTube and
Instagram Shorts rows were written with it before the fix.
