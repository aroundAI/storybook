## KB-169 — Revenue's not-measured reasons ignore the platform filter, and its hover gives the watch-time sentence

**Severity:** Low. A team whose revenue is not measured reads a reason
that is not about what it is looking at: a platform the filter has
removed, or a sentence about watch time and follower gain. No figure is
wrong; the explanation is. **Found:** 2026-10-02, recording the wave that
merged FILM-1726 (#523) and FILM-1709 (#529).

### What happens

**1. The reasons are the whole project's, whatever the filter says.**
The analytics page reads "Why revenue is not measured" once per project:
`useQuery({ queryKey: ['project-revenue-access', projectId] })` calls
`getProjectRevenueAccessAction({ projectId })`
(`packages/features/content-analytics/src/components/analytics-dashboard.tsx:142`).
The action returns a note for every connected channel on the account
(`packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/dashboard-actions.ts:170`),
and the page passes them to `MetricCards` as read
(`analytics-dashboard.tsx:350`). Neither takes FILM-1709's selection
(`platforms`, `analytics-dashboard.tsx:107`), and `MetricCards` lists
every note whenever the figure is null (`metric-cards.tsx:196`). So with
only TikTok selected, the list can still say YouTube's reason ("This
account was connected before we asked for permission to read its
earnings…").

**2. The revenue card's hover is about watch time and followers.**
`MetricCards` gives every null card the same `notMeasuredReason`, and the
revenue card is one of them (`metric-cards.tsx:332`, through
`unmeasuredReason`, `:295`). Its default is `PLATFORM_NOT_MEASURED_REASON`
(`metric-cards.tsx:50`, defaulted at `:94` and `:309`): "…not every
platform sends a watch time or a follower gain for a video". That is what
the episode page shows, which passes no reason. The analytics page passes
`NOT_COLLECTED_HERE_REASON` instead (`analytics-dashboard.tsx:347`,
defined at `metric-cards.tsx:54`): "Watch time and follower gain are not
collected for a whole project or season. Open an episode to see them." It
is the same mistake on both pages, and on the analytics page it is also
false: that page does read revenue for the project.

### Reproduced

A throwaway Vitest render of `MetricCards` (happy-dom, the mocks of
`metric-cards.test.tsx`), with `revenueCents: null`, read:

| Call | Revenue card `title` |
|---|---|
| No `notMeasuredReason` (episode page) | The platforms behind these figures did not report it: not every platform sends a watch time or a follower gain for a video. |
| `NOT_COLLECTED_HERE_REASON` (analytics page) | Watch time and follower gain are not collected for a whole project or season. Open an episode to see them. |

`revenueAccessNotes` over a YouTube connection with `youtube.revenue`
`scope_missing` and a TikTok one returned both notes, and `MetricCards`
listed both: it has no platform input, so a TikTok-only selection cannot
narrow them. The existing test `metric-cards.test.tsx:373` already counts
three "platforms behind these figures" titles, revenue's among them. Not
driven in the browser.

### Fix

- Pass the selection to the revenue-access read, or filter the notes on
  the client by `platforms`, so the list names only selected platforms.
- Give the revenue card its own not-measured sentence, pointing at the
  list under the cards, instead of the watch-time one.

### Acceptance criteria

- With only TikTok selected, "Why revenue is not measured" names no
  other platform; a test fails with the filter removed.
- The revenue card's hover never mentions watch time or follower gain, on
  the analytics or the episode page.
