## KB-207 — A deleted episode's scheduled posts still go out, and a video taken down on its platform keeps its channel's slot

> **Fixed (2026-10-19), #686.** A trigger on `episodes`
> (`cancel_deleted_episode_publishes`, migration
> `20261019132532_kb207-deleted-episode-publishes.sql`) deletes an episode's
> `scheduled` and `queued` publishes when `deleted_at` goes from null to a
> time; `publishing`, `published`, `failed` and `deleted` rows stay as
> history. The migration also cancels the posts of episodes deleted before
> it, which removes the production rows on deploy. Behind it: the
> scheduled-publish job selects only posts whose episode is not deleted
> (`episodes!inner` + `.is('episodes.deleted_at', null)`), and the publish
> worker fails a job whose episode is deleted with "Its episode was
> deleted, so it was not posted." The hourly analytics sync marks a
> `published` row `deleted`, with `metadata.removedAt` and
> `metadata.removedReason` ("not found on YouTube" etc.), only on a definite
> not-found: a YouTube id absent from a successful `videos.list`, or a Meta
> Graph answer of code 100 / subcode 33 or a 404 saying the object does not
> exist. Token, permission, rate-limit and server errors mark nothing. The
> Schedule tab offers "Mark as removed" on a published row (owners and
> admins, with a confirmation), shows `deleted` rows as "Removed" with the
> reason, and hides a deleted episode's posts unless "Deleted episodes" is
> ticked. MCP `cancel_scheduled_publish` checks that the publish is the
> team's rather than that its episode is live, and the new
> `mark_publish_removed` (studio:publish) does what the Schedule page does.
> Tests, red without the fix:
> `apps/web/supabase/tests/database/deleted-episode-publishes.test.sql`,
> `apps/web/lambda/scheduled-publish/__tests__/index.test.ts` ("does not send
> a post of a deleted episode"),
> `apps/web/lambda/publish-worker/__tests__/job-guard.test.ts` ("refuses to
> upload a publish of a deleted episode"),
> `packages/features/content-analytics/__tests__/youtube-live-counts.test.ts`,
> `packages/features/content-analytics/__tests__/meta-content-missing.test.ts`
> and `packages/features/studio-mcp/__tests__/tools/publish-removal-tools.test.ts`.

**Severity:** High: a deleted episode's long video would have been posted
to the owner's channels, and the re-uploaded episode could not be scheduled
in its place.

**Found:** 2026-10-19 in production. The owner deleted episodes and
re-uploaded them. Seven `scheduled` long-video rows of the deleted episodes
were still due, and scheduling the re-uploads was refused by the posting
rules (KB-204), which counted those rows and the `published` rows whose
posts the owner had already deleted on YouTube and Facebook. MCP
`cancel_scheduled_publish` answered "No episode with this id in your team
(deleted episodes are not visible)."

### What happened

- Episodes are soft-deleted (`episodes.deleted_at`) and nothing touched
  their `publishes`. The scheduled-publish job selected due `scheduled` rows
  with no look at the episode, so they would have been sent.
- `readCalendar` (the KB-204 posting-rules guard) counts `scheduled`,
  `queued` and `publishing` rows and `published` rows of the last 35 days,
  so the deleted episodes' rows held the channels' slots.
- MCP's cancel checked the episode through `requireEpisodeInAccount`, which
  hides deleted episodes, so the rows could not be cancelled over MCP.
- StoryBook never learnt that a published video was taken down on its
  platform. `publishes.status` already allowed `deleted`, and every reader
  that matters (the guard, the cadence gate's `postsToday`, the daily
  digest, the publish calendar) already leaves it out; nothing set it.

### Not covered

- Meta's code 100 / subcode 33 also covers "cannot be loaded due to missing
  permissions"; it is read as gone, as the fix specified. A Page token that
  lost access answers with the permission codes (10, 190, 200) instead.
- A YouTube video made private to a channel other than the connected one is
  also absent from `videos.list`; the connection is the channel's owner, so
  this only happens after a channel is reconnected as another channel.
