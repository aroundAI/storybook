## KB-132 — Reads under projects scan every personal account through the accounts policy, for a branch that can never match

> **Fixed (2026-09-27), #429.** One migration recreates the 22 policies from
> their live definitions with the personal-owner branch removed, nothing else
> changed. `kb132-personal-owner-branch.test.sql` fails if any policy outside
> `accounts` tests `is_personal_account`, or a project read plans a scan of
> personal accounts (both red without the migration). The same file, with the
> whole pgTAP suite (1437 tests), shows team access unchanged. Depends on KB-99
> (#426): merge that first.

**Severity:** Medium. The cost falls on every page that reads a project or
anything under one, and it grows with the number of users, since every user
has a personal account. **Found:** chasing #426's revenue evidence timeouts,
2026-09-27.

The read policies of `projects`, `episodes`, `publishes`, `revenue_records`,
`assets`, `seasons`, `shots`, `captions`, `dialogue_lines`, the thumbnails,
intros and the rest under a project (22 in all, including `episodes_update`,
`episodes_delete` and `shots_delete`) read:

```sql
exists (select 1 from accounts a
         where a.id = p.account_id
           and a.primary_owner_user_id = auth.uid()
           and a.is_personal_account = true)
or has_role_on_account(p.account_id)
```

### Reproduced (2026-09-27, lane B after #426's full E2E run)

`auto_explain` on PostgREST's own session, for the publish-scoped read in
`forEachAccountRevenueRow`:

```
duration: 4213.011 ms
  Seq Scan on projects p_3  (actual time=4210.229..4210.231 rows=1)
    SubPlan 35 -> Bitmap Heap Scan on accounts (actual time=2113.076..2113.077 rows=1)
                    Filter: (is_mfa_compliant() AND (is_super_admin() OR … OR has_role_on_account(id) OR is_account_team_member(id)) …)
                    Rows Removed by Filter: 332
    SubPlan 4  -> Bitmap Heap Scan on accounts (actual time=2096.197..2096.198 rows=1)
```

The subplan is the personal-owner branch: every personal account's id, read
through `accounts`' own policy. An A/B on that database failed 5 of 6
revenue and overview evidence tests on main and on #426 alike; on a fresh
database both passed 6/6.

**Not reproduced synthetically.** 400 seeded users on a fresh database kept
the same read at 2–3 ms without the fix (1.3–1.8 ms with it). What made each
row cost milliseconds in the accumulated database is not established; the fix
removes the step that paid for it.

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] No policy outside `accounts` tests `is_personal_account`, and a project read plans no personal-account scan (pgTAP, red without the migration)
- [x] Team access is unchanged: an owner reads the project and its episode, a stranger reads neither; the whole pgTAP suite passes (1437)
- [ ] The E2E evidence stage passes after a full E2E run, where #426's failed on the same specs (local CI run of #429)
