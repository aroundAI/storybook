## KB-112 — Voice cloning and voice listing spend the account's ElevenLabs key without a project-write check

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #393.** `startVoiceClone`, `deleteVoiceClone` and
> `listVoicesAction` ask `authorizeProjectTarget` (KB-31, `can_write_project` as
> the caller) for the asset's or the named project before the consent write,
> the key read or the ElevenLabs call, and take the account from the target.
> Listing was decided as a write: it is the first step of assigning a voice,
> both its callers (`VoiceAssignment`, `autoAssignVoicesAction`) assign, and it
> uses the key. Reproduced red on `main` in
> `packages/features/audio-generation/__tests__/voice-clone-authorization.test.ts`:
> all three reached the key and ElevenLabs for a reader (3 of 6 failed).

**Severity:** Low — only people with a role on the account can reach it, and
listing voices costs no credits; starting a clone does. **Found:** KB-84's
inventory of every `external_api_keys` reader (2026-09-25). Found by reading
the actions against #337's rule; not yet driven against a database.

#337 (KB-46) made nine ElevenLabs spend paths ask `can_write_project` as the
caller before the key is read. Three paths that also use the key were not
among them, and check only that the caller is signed in:

- `startVoiceClone` (`packages/features/audio-generation/src/server/voice-clone-actions.ts:37`)
  reads the asset, stores a consent record, marks the voice profile pending,
  and uploads samples to ElevenLabs on the account's key;
- `deleteVoiceClone` (same file, `:193`) deletes the cloned voice at
  ElevenLabs on the account's key;
- `listVoicesAction` (`voice-profile-actions.ts:41`) lists the account's
  ElevenLabs voices for any project the caller can read.

The key read itself is access-checked since KB-84 (the caller must have a
role on the account), so a stranger is refused. A project viewer, or a team
member with no role on the project, is not: they can start or delete a clone
on the team's key.

### Proposed fix

Ask `authorizeProjectTarget` (KB-31) for the asset's project in
`startVoiceClone` and `deleteVoiceClone` before the consent write and the key
read, as #337 did for SFX and music. Decide whether listing voices is a read
(any project reader) or a spend (project writers); it costs no credits.

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] Unit: a caller who can read but not write the asset's project is refused by `startVoiceClone` and `deleteVoiceClone`, and neither the key nor ElevenLabs is reached (`voice-clone-authorization.test.ts`; `listVoicesAction` too)
- [x] Mutation guards for both checks (`tooling/mutation-guards/kb-112-118-119.json`, VS1–VS3)
