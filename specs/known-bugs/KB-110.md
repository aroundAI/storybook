## KB-110 — Regenerate on a finished ElevenLabs music track calls the retired Suno action and adds a failed track

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #386.** FILM-514 removed Suno. The Music timeline now offers Regenerate only on a music cue, which regenerates through ElevenLabs as before. A finished track can still be played, re-prompted, moved and deleted. `apps/e2e/tests/studio/music-timeline.spec.ts` asserts it, and mutation guard E1 in `tooling/mutation-guards/film-514.json` goes red when Regenerate is offered on every track again.

**Severity:** Low — it affected anyone who clicked Regenerate on a music track that ElevenLabs had generated. Nothing was lost, but each click could leave a failed track on the timeline. **Found:** FILM-514 (retiring Suno), while tracing every caller of the Suno actions.

ElevenLabs music inserts `audio_tracks(type='music')` with `metadata.sceneNumber` and no `isCue` (`core/elevenlabs-music-core.ts`, `elevenlabs-music-actions.ts`). The timeline's Regenerate handler treated any track with a scene number and no `isCue` as "scene music" and called `generateSceneMusicAction`, which is Suno-only. `SUNO_API_KEY` is in no deployment configuration, so that action inserted a pending track, failed, and marked it failed. The original track stayed.

**Reproduced** on `main` (`ddc5568a`), local stack, dev server. I seeded an episode whose screenplay has scene 1, plus one finished ElevenLabs-shaped music track. Selecting it and clicking Regenerate left two rows for the episode:

| Row | name | file_url | metadata |
|---|---|---|---|
| seeded | Harbour theme at dusk | `https://example.com/harbour-theme.mp3` | `{prompt, sceneNumber: 1}` |
| added by Regenerate | Scene 1 Music | null | `{status: "failed", error: "SUNO_API_KEY not configured"}` |

Pointing Regenerate at ElevenLabs instead would not have regenerated anything. `generateMusicElevenLabsAction` answers a repeated prompt from the asset library, so it would have returned the same audio.

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] A finished non-cue music track offers no Regenerate; a music cue does (`music-timeline.spec.ts`, red on `main`, green after)
- [x] A finished non-cue music track can still be deleted (same spec)
- [x] E1 mutation guard RED
