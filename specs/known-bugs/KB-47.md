## KB-47 — The voice, publish and render queues were not surveyed for caller-supplied ids

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #384 (publish; voice in #337).** Reproduced on `main`
> (`ddc5568a`, main's migrations in a throwaway Postgres): a project viewer and an
> account member off the project read the episode's publishes, their update to
> `deleting` matched no row, and `unpublishAction` / `deleteEpisodePublishesAction`
> sent the delete job anyway; the publish worker then deleted the video on the
> platform with the connection's token. For a writer the update failed too:
> `deleting` was not an allowed `publishes.status`, and the error was ignored.
>
> **Owner decision (2026-09-25): only project owners and admins may take a
> published video down** (`TAKEDOWN_ROLES` in `@kit/publishing/lib/takedown`, the
> one list the actions, the worker and the Publish screen read). Members get "Only
> project owners and admins can take a published video down.", returned as a
> value, and do not see the controls. The actions queue only the rows their
> update marked (`deleting` is now a status). The worker re-reads the row,
> requires `deleting` and the role, and deletes the row's video with the row's
> connection, never the message's. Guards WA7–WA15, DB WA18.

**Severity:** High if any worker trusts a payload id the way the LLM worker did.
**Found:** KB-31 (2026-09-23) surveyed only `queueLlmJob`. **Voice part fixed**
in #337 (KB-46). **Publish part open.**

These producers send SQS messages to workers that run on the service-role key,
and were not checked for an authorising read before the send:

- ~~`packages/features/audio-generation/src/server/voice-queue-helper.ts:90`~~ **Fixed
  (KB-46, #337).** Reproduced: a project viewer or a teammate with no
  project role could queue voice for a line they cannot edit, on the team's key.
  The worker writes the audio. All three producers now authorise with
  `can_write_project`, and the queue requires the target.
- ~~`packages/features/publishing/src/server/publish-actions.ts:960`, `:1039`~~ **Fixed
  (#384).** Reproduced; see the banner. The two producers are
  `deleteEpisodePublishesAction` and `unpublishHandler`; the third producer,
  the `scheduled-publish` cron, reads its ids from `publishes` rows (KB-109)
- ~~`packages/features/edit-suite/src/server/render-actions.ts:146`, `:226`~~ — removed with the
  Edit Suite (FILM-607, #329). It authorised by account membership, and an
  RLS update matching no row still sent the SQS message (KB-32's report)

**Proposed fix (publish):** list each producer with the read that authorises its
target, as KB-31 did; give the queue a typed target like `queueLlmJob`'s and
`queueVoiceJob`'s.
