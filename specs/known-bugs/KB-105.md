## KB-105 — Updates whose success is shown are not checked for affected rows; RLS filters a refused one to none, silently

> **Fixed (2026-09-28), #462.** The last criterion: one browser case, in a
> production build. A team member removed from the team in another tab picks
> a new language for a channel; RLS matches the update to no row, and the
> page says "You can't change this channel's language." instead of "Language
> set to Spanish" (`refusals.po.ts`, 3 of 3). With `requireAffectedRows`
> removed from the action the case fails: no error toast. Deleting the
> connection instead is not possible (KB-22's trigger refuses it).
>
> **Fixed in part (2026-09-25), #409.** Every unchecked `.update()` and
> `.upsert()` (160: 149 updates, 11 upserts) is classified in the pull request.
> **59** show their success to the user, or gate a paid call or a queued job.
> The other 101 are listed with a reason: 44 service role, admin or lambda,
> 15 failure marks, 13 records of a result, 8 upserts, and the rest cleanup,
> bookkeeping or dead code.
>
> **Upserts are not affected:** an upsert that RLS refuses *raises* ("new row
> violates row-level security policy"). This was measured on the local
> database for DO UPDATE, DO NOTHING and RETURNING, as a stranger to the row;
> the owner's row was unchanged. That holds where the insert policy refuses
> the caller. A DO NOTHING upsert by someone who may insert but not update
> skips silently, but "already there" is its intended success.
>
> This PR converts **18** of the 59: web app (canon settings, shot upload),
> public-sharing (5), team-accounts (invitation role and renew, team
> picture ×2), accounts (picture ×2, account details), assets (2),
> projects (1) and notifications (1). `kb105-unchecked-updates.test.ts`
> fails any new unchecked plain update. The remaining **40** are listed there
> as pending, in three per-package PRs (audio-generation 17, episodes 14,
> publishing 9). The tag rename is left to tag-scope's open PR.
>
> **#415 (audio-generation):** 14 more. The paid gates (`generating` before
> TTS, the batch retry before dispatch, the cue before its LLM job) now refuse
> before anything is spent or queued. The bulk and auto voice assignments
> report each character by whether its row changed. The voice-clone pair is
> left to #393, which rewrites those functions, and the lip-sync update to
> #371, which deletes the file.
>
> **#442 (episodes):** all 14. Twelve now check what they changed: the
> publish screen's video and shorts saves, asset links, refinements, episode
> edits, canon threads and settings, continuity, and the thumbnail pick. The
> other two, `autoStitchAction` and `planTimelineAction`, had no caller and
> were deleted with `lib/auto-stitch.ts` (owner's decision, 2026-09-27).
>
> **#449 (publishing, the clone gate, the tag rename):** every update left.
> Six check what they changed: a channel's language, a project's publishing
> settings (whose save button now shows the refusal; it showed nothing
> either way), a retry's and a social post's `publishing` mark before the
> platform call, a social post's approval, and a voice clone's `pending`
> mark before the ElevenLabs call. Three are dead and labelled so in the
> scan: the episode publishing settings' two (their component is exported
> and rendered nowhere) and the tag rename (no caller). `queueDeleteJobs`'s
> update puts a publish's status back when its delete message did not send,
> a recovery, not a success. #371 deleted the lip-sync file and its update.
> #451 then deleted the three dead ones with the code around them.
>
> **Open:** the browser case below — done in #462, above.

**Severity:** Low — the user is told a change was saved when the database
refused it; nothing is lost, and a reload shows the truth. **Found:** KB-61
(2026-09-25), which fixed the same shape for deletes and soft deletes.

An UPDATE whose `USING` clause matches no row is answered
`{ data: [], error: null }`, as one that worked. KB-61 checked every delete
and soft delete whose success is shown (`requireAffectedRows`,
`@kit/next/refusals`). Plain updates were left: of 160 user-side
`.update(` statements in `apps/web/app` and `packages`, 136 have no
`.select(` (counted 2026-09-25 by joining each `.update(` to its
`.from('<table>')` and statement end; lambdas and scripts excluded). Most are
status and progress writes nobody is told about; some are edits with a
success toast.

### Reproduced (2026-09-25, `main` ddc5568a)

Local database, rolled-back transaction, as a project **viewer** of the
episode's project: `update public.episodes set deleted_at = now() where id =
<episode>` → **0 rows, no error** (see KB-61's DB check). The same holds for
any update the viewer's role cannot make; the actions that send such an
update and toast success on it are what this entry is about.

### Proposed fix

Sweep the 136 for those whose outcome is shown, and give each
`.select('id')` + `requireAffectedRows`, in the actions' `returnRefusals`
wrapper; extend `packages/next/__tests__/unchecked-deletes.ts` to plain
updates with an allowlist for the rest, as KB-61 did.

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] One browser case: a refused edit reads as refused, in a production build (#462)
