## KB-205 — A video over 15 minutes to an unverified YouTube channel is marked published while YouTube abandons it as too long

> **Fixed (2026-10-19), #PRNUM.** Four parts:
>
> - **The status is stored.** The YouTube connect callback asks
>   `channels.list` for `part=status` too, and stores
>   `status.longUploadsStatus` (`allowed | eligible | disallowed |
>   longUploadsUnspecified`) as `metadata.long_uploads_status` with
>   `long_uploads_checked_at`, for a single channel and for one chosen in
>   the channel picker. A token refresh reads it again
>   (`ensureValidToken`, at most every six hours from the cron, always from
>   Refresh in Settings); a failed read keeps what was stored and the
>   refresh still succeeds. 1 quota unit a read.
> - **The preflight refuses.** `preparePublish`
>   (`publish-preflight.ts`), which publish now, schedule,
>   `schedule_release` and the MCP tools all run, refuses a video longer
>   than 15:00 to a YouTube channel whose status is not `allowed`, naming
>   the channel and https://www.youtube.com/verify. A channel connected
>   before this fix has no status: the video is refused too, saying the
>   status is unknown and to press Refresh. The file is read only for such a
>   channel; a verified one costs nothing.
> - **Settings → Platforms says so.** A YouTube channel not `allowed` shows
>   "Long uploads: not verified (15-minute limit)" with a Verify link; one
>   never checked shows "Long uploads: not checked yet. Press Refresh to
>   check".
> - **The worker's result is checked.** `checkYouTubeProcessing`, run by
>   the 30-minute token-refresh cron, asks `videos.list
>   part=processingDetails,status` (1 unit per channel per 50 videos) about
>   each YouTube publish of the last 48 hours not yet settled. One
>   `rejected` (e.g. `rejectionReason: length`), `failed`, or with
>   `processingStatus` `failed`/`terminated` is marked failed with
>   YouTube's reason; a processed one is recorded as settled and not asked
>   about again.
>
> Tests, red without the fix: `publish-platform-limits.test.ts` (the 15:12
> video refused to an `eligible`, `disallowed`, `longUploadsUnspecified`
> or never-read channel; let through to `allowed`, and at exactly 15:00),
> `token-refresh.test.ts` (the refresh stores the connected channel's
> status, not another of the account's), and `youtube-processing.test.ts`
> (the verdicts, and the job marking a length rejection failed). The vendor
> sandbox serves `part=status` (`allowed`) and `processingDetails`
> (`succeeded`).

**Severity:** High: a release reported as out was not on YouTube. Every
video over 15 minutes to an unverified channel was lost this way, and
nothing in StoryBook said so.

**Found:** 2026-10-19, in production. Two long videos (over 15:00) were
uploaded by StoryBook to unverified channels. YouTube Studio showed
"Processing abandoned — Video is too long"; StoryBook marked both
publishes `published`. A 15:12 (912 s) video got through the FILM-2212
preflight, which had no YouTube length limit (YouTube's own limit,
256 GB / 12 hours, is above StoryBook's).

**Cause:** YouTube's 15-minute limit is per channel: it lifts once the
channel is verified (https://www.youtube.com/verify). StoryBook read
neither the channel's `status.longUploadsStatus` nor, after `videos.insert`
returned an id, what YouTube then did with the upload: an id means the
bytes arrived, not that the video was processed.

**After the fix is deployed:** the cron checks uploads of the last 48
hours, so the two publishes from 2026-10-19 are marked failed if the fix
is deployed within 48 hours of their upload; otherwise they stay
`published`. Publish them again once the channels are verified. Existing YouTube connections have no stored status until their
next refresh (within six hours, or Refresh in Settings); until then a
video over 15 minutes to them is refused with the "status unknown" message.
