## KB-149 — Episode analytics page shows an unmeasured watch time and follower gain as 0m and 0

> **Fixed (2026-10-01), #TBD.** `getEpisodeAnalytics` adds a publish's
> watch time, follower gain and saves only where ClickHouse's per-video
> `measured` flag (KB-114) says it was measured, so a TikTok-only episode
> has `null` for all three, and watch time is averaged over the publishes
> that measured it — it divided by every publish, which halved a YouTube
> figure beside a TikTok one. `subscribersGained` is now on the episode
> analytics and passed to the cards instead of a literal `0`.
> `MetricCards` takes `number | null` for Watch Time and Subscribers and
> renders null as "Not measured" (`data-test="metric-not-measured"`), the
> reach overview's wording, with the reason as its title.
>
> The same class, fixed with it: the project, season and analytics
> dashboards passed a hard-coded `0` for both ("not available at project
> level"); they pass `null`, and the card's reason says the figure is not
> collected at that level. The analytics dashboard also sends those totals
> to the AI insights job, whose payload schema (`llm-job-payloads.ts`,
> `insights-actions.ts`) now accepts null — it would have refused the job —
> and whose change calculation skips a null rather than reporting −100%
> (the skip is #504's, for Facebook views; KB-149's guard names it).
>
> This also fixes KB-154 ("AI Insights is sent a literal 0 for project
> watch time and subscribers"): the analytics dashboard's `totals`, which
> `AIInsights` sends to `generateInsightsAction`, carry `null` for both,
> the two payload schemas accept it, and the job sends the model `null`
> with no change figure for either (`analytics-insights-handler.test.ts`,
> the KB-149 case). The job states the key as `null` rather than leaving it
> out of the summary. KB-154 is filed with FILM-1705 and closed when that
> lands.
>
> Evidence: `episode-analytics-unmeasured.test.ts`, the KB-149 cases in
> `metric-cards.test.tsx` and `analytics-insights-handler.test.ts`, each
> red before the fix; `sandbox-sync.spec.ts`'s TikTok test asserts both
> cards read "Not measured" (screenshot `sync-1-tiktok-episode.png`), and
> its Instagram test that a Reel's watch time is still a figure while
> Subscribers reads "Not measured" (`sync-2-instagram-reel.png`). An
> Instagram Reel does report watch time (FILM-1712); the entry's "or
> Instagram" holds for follower gain, and for watch time on a post that
> is not a Reel. Six guards in `tooling/mutation-guards/kb-149.json`.
>
> Not fixed here: the account dashboard (`account-dashboard-actions.ts`)
> reads `queryTotals`, which sums NULL watch time to 0 with no `measured`
> flag, so an all-TikTok account still shows Watch Time 0m. Fixing it is a
> change to that ClickHouse query.

**Severity:** Low — every creator who opens a TikTok (or Instagram) episode's
analytics page reads "Watch Time 0m" and "Subscribers 0" where the platform
reported nothing. A 0 reads as "nobody watched", which is a measurement the
data does not support (phase 17's "null means cannot measure"). **Found:**
FILM-1804's sandbox sync flow.

**Reproduced** (FILM-1804, `apps/e2e/tests/analytics/sandbox-sync.spec.ts`,
TikTok test): a TikTok video synced from the sandbox stores `saves` and
`watch_time_seconds` as NULL in `video_metrics` — the test asserts it, as
migration 014 intends — and the page
`/home/[account]/studio/[project]/episodes/[episode]/analytics` renders the
Watch Time card as "0m" and Subscribers as "0" (evidence screenshot
`sync-1-tiktok-episode.png`).

Cause: `getEpisodeAnalytics` (packages/features/content-analytics/src/server/aggregation-queries.ts)
sums `stats.watch_time_seconds` into a number that starts at 0, and
`EpisodeAnalytics` passes `subscribersGained: 0` outright; `MetricCards`
then shows `value || 0`. No null reaches the card.

**Fix:** carry null through the episode totals (a sum over no measured
publish is null) and let `MetricCards` render an absent value the way the
Video Log does — the reason it is unmeasured, not a number.

**Acceptance:** the sandbox sync flow's TikTok episode page shows Watch Time
and Subscribers as not measured, and a YouTube episode with real watch time
still shows its figure.
