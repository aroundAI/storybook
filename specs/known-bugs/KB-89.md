## KB-89 — Episode thumbnails cannot be deleted in the app: their settings panel is rendered nowhere

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #PR.** Owner decisions of 2026-09-25: render the
> panel (keeping custom language codes and "set default"), and let project
> members remove thumbnails.
>
> - `EpisodeThumbnailSettings` is on the episode's **publish screen**,
>   below the full videos — there is no episode settings page, and the
>   publish screen is where thumbnails are chosen and used. It no longer
>   loads its own copy: it reads and writes the publish screen's list, the
>   one the per-language video slots read, so the two cannot disagree about
>   which thumbnail is current (asserted: after a remove, the slot shows the
>   default).
> - Removing asks for confirmation, removes the row first and then the file
>   (KB-61), and a remove that removed nothing says so.
> - `episode_thumbnails_delete` follows `can_write_project`, like the
>   table's insert and update and the storage policy on `episodes/<E>/`
>   (migration `20260925104159_kb89-thumbnail-delete-project-write.sql`,
>   pgTAP `episode-thumbnails-delete-rls.test.sql`, red first).
> - The panel's uploads use the shared path builder and the publish screen's
>   2MB limit (it had its own path and a 5MB limit).

**Severity:** Low. **Found:** KB-54 (2026-09-24). **Reproduced** by search on
`main` 52ed2ade: `EpisodeThumbnailSettings`
(`…/studio/episodes/[episodeSlug]/_components/episode-thumbnail-settings.tsx:57`)
is imported by no file in `apps/` or `packages/`. So
`deleteEpisodeThumbnailAction` has no caller, and neither do the panel's
set-default or per-language add. The publish screen (`publish-screen.tsx:260`)
can upload and replace a thumbnail per language, but nothing lets a creator
remove one. Reproduced again on `main` ddc5568a on 2026-09-25.

**Fixed** in #PR — see the banner.
