## KB-193 — The project overview shows +12%, +5% and a rising trend line that nothing measured

> **Fixed (2026-10-05), #644.** `PerformanceInsightsSection` no longer
> renders the badge beside each figure or the `Sparkline` under two of
> them. No card has a measured previous period to compare against, so none
> shows a change. `apps/web/app/home/[account]/studio/[projectSlug]/_components/__tests__/performance-insights-section.test.tsx`
> fails on main (finds `+12%`, `+5%`, and the canned curve) and passes
> after; `apps/e2e/tests/studio/overview-trend.spec.ts` does the same in the
> browser, on a seeded project with one published video (main read `+5%`).
> Screenshots are in the PR.

**Severity:** Medium — every project's overview page shows a growth figure
and a growth curve that were typed into the component. A creator reads
"+12%" as "views are up 12%". **Found:** KB-149, KB-162 and KB-192 settled
that a figure we did not measure is never shown as a number; this was the
same rule broken on the project overview.

**What happens.** `performance-insights-section.tsx`, mounted by
`overview-content.tsx` on `/home/<team>/studio/<project>`, rendered:

- `{analytics?.totalViews ? '+12%' : '0%'}` beside Total Views: any
  non-zero view count printed "+12%", and anything else "0%";
- `{analytics?.contentCount ? '+5%' : '0%'}` beside Content Published, the
  same way: a project with one published video read "+5%";
- a literal `0%` beside Avg. Engagement;
- `<Sparkline>` under Total Views and Content Published, a fixed SVG path
  (`M0 20 Q 20 18, 40 10 T 100 5`) that rises whatever the data.

Nothing computes a previous period for any of the three cards.
`OverviewAnalytics` carries one period's totals. Reproduced on a seeded
project: main read "Total Views Not measured 0% … Content Published 1 +5%"
with the curve drawn; after, no badge and no curve.

**Fix.** Remove the badges and the sparklines. A real change is shown only
once a measured period-over-period figure exists for a card.

**Same class, not touched here.** `performance-insights.tsx` (a
`PerformanceInsights` component with canned sparkline paths) and
`dashboard-demo-charts.tsx` (`Trend` badges of 20%, 12%, 9%) are not
imported by any page. Neither was changed.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [x] The Performance Insights cards render no signed percentage and no
  trend line. Unit and Playwright guards, each red on main.
- [x] The figures that are measured (views, engagement, content count) are
  still shown.
