## KB-97 — Music and SFX generation neither check nor count the account budget

> **Fixed (2026-09-25), #374: resolved by removal.** The owner decided
> to remove the budget machinery ("Remove the budget machinery"), so no
> generation checks or counts a budget: music, SFX and voice alike. Before
> the decision, the paths that spent without counting were five, not four:
> the LLM worker's cue generation (`generateSfxCore`,
> `generateMusicElevenLabsCore`) also spent the project's ElevenLabs key.
> Suno music is being removed separately.

**Severity:** Low while no account sets a budget. **Found:** KB-83
(2026-09-24), by reading. **Open.**

`generateMusicAssetAction` and `generateSfxAssetAction`
(`packages/features/audio-generation/src/server/audio-asset-actions.ts`), and
the actions in `sfx-actions.ts`, `elevenlabs-music-actions.ts` and
`music-actions.ts`, spend the account's ElevenLabs (or Suno) key but never
call `check_account_budget` or `increment_account_usage`
(`git grep` finds both only in the voice paths). A budget therefore caps
voice and nothing else.

### Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Music and SFX refuse when over budget and record their cost through `recordVoiceSpend`'s pattern (a shared `recordSpend(target, cents)`), unit-tested red first
