## KB-209 — Scheduling a 1.07 GB long video to Facebook is refused, though Facebook takes it

> **Fixed (2026-10-19), #688.** `platform-video-limits.ts` now holds
> Facebook's resumable limit (1.75 GB, 45 minutes) and `FACEBOOK_CONSTRAINTS`
> reads it, so preflight and the provider share one source. The provider
> measures a URL by HEAD; up to 1 GB (`FACEBOOK_FILE_URL_MAX_BYTES`, decimal,
> as Meta writes it) Meta fetches it by `file_url`, above that the provider
> reads ranged chunks from the URL and sends them by the 3-phase resumable
> protocol. Tests, red without the fix:
> `packages/features/publishing/__tests__/facebook-large-video.test.ts` and
> `publish-platform-limits.test.ts`.

**Symptom:** scheduling a 1.07 GB long video to Facebook failed with "Facebook
can't take this video: it is 1.07 GB, and Facebook takes at most 1 GB."

**Severity:** Medium. A long video between 1 and 1.75 GB could not be
posted to Facebook; every other platform and every smaller file was
unaffected.

**Cause:** FILM-2212 (#677) quoted Meta's limit for a multi-part or URL upload
("cannot exceed 1 GB in size and 20 minutes"). Meta's resumable upload takes
"up to 1.75GB and 45 minutes"
(https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/event/videos/).
Raising only the preflight would have moved the failure to the worker: the
publish lambda set `fetchFromUrl`, which sent every size by `file_url`, and
the provider's resumable threshold was 1 GiB (1.0737e9), above 1.07e9, so
a 1.07 GB file would still have gone by `file_url`. The provider's own
maximum, 10 GiB, was a figure Meta's pages do not state.

**Not found:** a larger Pages limit (10 GB, 240 minutes) is not in Meta's
Graph API pages read (event/videos, page/videos, video-api overview and
publishing guide, graph-api/video-uploads); the documented figure is used.
StoryBook posts every Facebook video, Shorts included, as a regular Page
video, never a Reel, so the Reels limits do not enter. A file of 1.75 GB
streams through the worker in 64 MB chunks; the worker's time limit is
untested against a file that size.
