# Initial Concept
StoryBook is the complete AI-powered film studio for creators and production teams. Manage your entire pipeline—from story ideation to YouTube publishing—in one collaborative workspace.

# Product Guide: StoryBook

## Product Vision
StoryBook is an end-to-end AI film studio designed to democratize high-quality video production. By integrating best-in-class generative AI models for text, video, and audio into a unified, collaborative workspace, StoryBook enables creators to move from a raw concept to a published, multi-platform video hit without needing disjointed tools or deep technical expertise.

## Target Audience
- **Solo Content Creators:** YouTubers, TikTokers, and Influencers looking to scale high-quality content production.
- **Independent Filmmakers:** Small teams and indie directors needing to visualize scripts, create storyboards, or produce animated films on a budget.
- **Marketing Agencies:** Teams managing social media for brands who need to produce consistent, engaging video content at scale.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Unified Workflow:** Replaces a fragmented stack of 5+ tools (scriptwriting, video gen, editing, audio, social scheduling) with a single "5-Tab Workflow".
- **Model Agnostic Power:** Leverage the best AI models for each task (Deepseek for story, Kling for video, ElevenLabs for voice) without vendor lock-in.
- **Production-Ready Output:** Goes beyond simple "text-to-video" clips by offering granular control over characters, consistency, shot composition, and audio syncing.

## Key Features
### 1. Pre-Production (The Bible)
- **Character Bank:** Define consistent character appearances, personalities, and voices.
- **World Building:** Store location assets and aesthetic guidelines.
- **Season Planning:** AI-assisted roadmap generation for episodic content.

### 2. The 5-Tab Production Studio
- **Story:** AI-driven ideation and industry-standard screenplay generation.
- **Visuals:** Auto-extraction of shot lists from scripts; AI video generation with state tracking.
- **Audio:** Automated dialogue extraction, voice cloning, and AI music/SFX generation.
- **Edit:** Multi-track timeline for stitching video and audio into a cohesive narrative.
- **Publish:** Integrated multi-platform scheduling and analytics dashboard.

### 3. Post-Launch Analytics
- Unified dashboard for cross-platform performance (YouTube, TikTok, etc.).
- Audience retention curves and revenue tracking.

## Monetization & Business Model
- **SaaS Subscription:** Tiered access based on usage (storage, generation minutes, team seats).
- **Team Collaboration:** Shared workspaces and permission management for agencies and production houses.
