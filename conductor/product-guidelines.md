# Product Guidelines: StoryBook

## Brand Personality
**Playful & Creative:** StoryBook is a playground for imagination. While it handles complex AI tasks, the experience should feel light, experimental, and inspiring. We want to lower the "fear of the blank page" and turn the filmmaking process into a series of creative discoveries.

## Voice & Tone
- **Friendly & Conversational:** We speak to users as partners in their creative process. We use "we" and "you" to build a relationship. (e.g., "Ready to see your characters in action?")
- **Succinct & Empowering:** While we are friendly, we respect the user's time. UI copy should be brief and lead to immediate action. We prefer clarity over cleverness when a user is in the middle of a workflow.
- **Encouraging:** AI can sometimes produce unexpected results. Our tone should frame these as "creative variations" or opportunities to iterate, rather than errors.

## Visual Identity Principles
- **Vibrant & Inspiring:** Use a color palette that feels alive and creative (Indigo, Pink, Cyan) rather than clinical or overly corporate.
- **Accessible Complexity:** filmmaking is complex. Our UI should use progressive disclosure—showing the simple paths first while keeping advanced controls available for "pro" moments.
- **Consistency is King:** Since we are a multi-stage studio (5 tabs), the visual language (icons, buttons, spacing) must remain identical across all modules to reduce cognitive load.

## User Experience Goals
- **The "Magic" Moment:** Aim to get the user to their first AI-generated "wow" (e.g., seeing their character for the first time) within the first 3 minutes of use.
- **Seamless Continuity:** Transitioning from script to shot list to audio should feel like one continuous stream, not switching between separate apps.
- **Creative Safety:** Always allow for easy "undo" or "regenerate." The user should never feel like they've "wasted" their turn or broken their project.
